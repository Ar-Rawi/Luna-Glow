## 1. Overview & Seasonal Filtering Engine

Malaysian waters span three primary marine hydro-zones: **The Straits of Malacca (West Coast)**, **The South China Sea (East Coast & Sarawak/Sabah)**, and **The Sulu & Celebes Seas (East Sabah)**.

- **Northeast Monsoon (Nov – Mar):** Heavy swells & rough seas on the East Coast; West Coast (Straits of Malacca) is sheltered, clear, and highly productive for estuary & surf fishing (Barramundi, Threadfin, Snapper).
- **Southwest Monsoon (May – Sep):** **GOLDEN SEASON FOR THE EAST COAST!** Glassy seas, crystal clear water, prime offshore trolling (Tenggiri, Layaran, GT, Amberjack, Coral Trout).
- **Inter-Monsoon (Apr & Oct):** Glassy sea conditions nationwide. Peak pelagic migration & oil platform jigging.

## 2. Exhaustive 50-Species Catalog by Family & Habitat

### 🐟 Category A: Estuary & Mangrove Predators

#### 1. Barramundi (*Lates calcarifer* | Siakap / Siakap Batu)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February, March (West Coast Peak)
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 1m – 15m
- **Tactics:** Target first 2 hours of Incoming Flood Tide around mangrove root drains and jetty pilings. Use live prawns, live mullet, or 100mm-120mm deep diving minnows.

#### 2. Mangrove Red Snapper (*Lutjanus argentimaculatus* | Siakap Merah / Flamboyan)
- **Peak Months:** March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** 2m – 20m
- **Tactics:** Aggressive ambush predator in mangrove holes and submerged timber. Use 40lb fluorocarbon leader, live prawns, or weedless soft plastic lures.

#### 3. Golden Snapper (*Lutjanus johnii* | Jenahak / Tanda)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 10m – 40m
- **Tactics:** Cautious deep channel bottom feeder. Target rocky bommies with live prawns or fresh squid tentacles on running sinker rigs.

#### 4. Fourfinger Threadfin (*Eleutheronema tetradactylum* | Kurau)
- **Peak Months:** November, December, January, February, March (West Coast Surf)
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 29°C | **Depth:** 1m – 10m
- **Tactics:** Stalks muddy tide lines near estuary beaches. Light tackle with live small prawns or silver micro-jigs.

#### 5. Indian Threadfin (*Polydactylus indicus* | Senangin)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 1m – 8m
- **Tactics:** Shallow muddy bays and beach surf. Target with live glass shrimp or light bottom float rigs.

#### 6. Tripletail (*Lobotes surinamensis* | Kakap Batu)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** Surface – 15m
- **Tactics:** Mimics floating leaves near mangrove flotsam and marker buoys. Sight-cast live prawn under a small float.

### 🐟 Category B: Pelagics & Gamefish (Tuna, Mackerel, Billfish)

#### 7. Spanish Mackerel (*Scomberomorus commerson* | Tenggiri Batang)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October (East Coast Peak)
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** Surface – 40m
- **Tactics:** Fast pelagic predator with razor teeth. Trolling minnows at 6 knots or wire-leader live baiting with live tamban (sardines).

#### 8. Spotted Spanish Mackerel (*Scomberomorus guttatus* | Tenggiri Papan)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** Surface – 25m
- **Tactics:** Schooling inshore mackerel. Light trolling with small silver spoons or high-speed minnow retrieve.

#### 9. Indo-Pacific Sailfish (*Istiophorus platypterus* | Layaran)
- **Peak Months:** July, August, September, October (Kuala Rompin Peak)
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** Surface – 30m
- **Tactics:** World famous in Kuala Rompin! Live selar baiting on circle hooks under drift floats. High speed aerial jumps.

#### 10. Yellowfin Tuna (*Thunnus albacares* | Tongkol Kapor)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September (Sabah / Layang-Layang)
- **Ideal SST:** 26°C – 29°C | **Depth:** Surface – 100m
- **Tactics:** Deep oceanic drop-offs and seamounts. Heavy surface popping or live bait drifting.

#### 11. Longtail Tuna (*Thunnus tonggol* | Tongkol Hitam)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** Surface – 50m
- **Tactics:** Coastal tuna shoals in South China Sea. Casting 30g–50g silver metal jigs into boiling surface frenzies.

#### 12. Kawakawa (*Euthynnus affinis* | Tongkol Kurik)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 31°C | **Depth:** Surface – 40m
- **Tactics:** Aggressive surface schools. Fast speed retrieving of small metal jigs or feathers.

#### 13. Mahi-Mahi / Dorado (*Coryphaena hippurus* | Belitung)
- **Peak Months:** July, August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** Surface – 30m
- **Tactics:** FADs (Rumpon) and floating debris. Light trolling with skirts or live bait drift.

#### 14. Wahoo (*Acanthocybium solandri* | Tenggiri Giri)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September
- **Ideal SST:** 26°C – 29°C | **Depth:** Surface – 50m
- **Tactics:** Deep ocean drop-offs around islands. High speed trolling (8–10 knots) with wire leader lures.

### 🐟 Category C: Trevallies & Carangids (Jemuduk, Ebek, Cermin)

#### 15. Giant Trevally (*Caranx ignobilis* | Jemuduk / Ebek)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 5m – 60m
- **Tactics:** Hunts in heavy current rips at capes and oil rigs. Work 160mm topwater poppers aggressively during Solunar Major Overhead periods.

#### 16. Bigeye Trevally (*Caranx sexfasciatus* | Cupak)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 5m – 40m
- **Tactics:** Night pack hunter around lit oil rigs and drop-offs. Light rock fishing jigs or minnow lures.

#### 17. Bluefin Trevally (*Caranx melampygus* | Cupak Biru)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 2m – 30m
- **Tactics:** Vibrant electric blue fins on coral reefs. Fast topwater walking-the-dog stickbaits.

#### 18. Golden Trevally (*Gnathanodon speciosus* | Cermin)
- **Peak Months:** March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 5m – 35m
- **Tactics:** Fleshy rubbery mouth feeding on sand crustaceans. Micro-jigging or live shrimp bottom rigs.

#### 19. Diamond Trevally (*Alectis ciliaris* | Ebek Cermin)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 10m – 50m
- **Tactics:** Deep reef current edges. Slow pitch metal jigs (60g–120g) on light braided line.

### 🐟 Category D: Groupers & Reef Snappers (Kerapu & Merah)

#### 20. Coral Trout (*Plectropomus maculatus* | Kerapu Sunoh / Kerapu Bintang)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 10m – 50m
- **Tactics:** Highly prized red coral grouper. Slow pitch jigging or bottom live baiting. Lock drag to prevent hole retreat.

#### 21. Estuary Grouper (*Epinephelus coioides* | Kerapu Lumpur)
- **Peak Months:** Year-Round (Peak: Apr – Nov)
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** 2m – 30m
- **Tactics:** Muddy estuaries and harbor breakwaters. Heavy bottom baiting with live crab or mullet.

#### 22. Giant Grouper (*Epinephelus lanceolatus* | Kerapu Pertang)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 15m – 100m
- **Tactics:** The titan of deep wrecks. Heavy 100lb+ tackle with whole live stingray or big mullet bait.

#### 23. Malabar Red Snapper (*Lutjanus malabaricus* | Merah)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 26°C – 29°C | **Depth:** 20m – 80m
- **Tactics:** Deep offshore reefs. Bottom ledgering with squid or selar fillet.

#### 24. Emperor Red Snapper (*Lutjanus sebae* | Merah Coret)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 26°C – 29°C | **Depth:** 25m – 90m
- **Tactics:** Iconic red and white vertical stripes. Deep reef slow jigging or heavy bottom baiting.

### 🦑 Category E: Cephalopods & Coastal Game (Sotong & Pompano)

#### 25. Bigfin Reef Squid (*Sepioteuthis lessoniana* | Sotong Torak)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October (Night Peak)
- **Ideal SST:** 27°C – 30°C | **Depth:** 2m – 20m
- **Tactics:** Night light squid fishing around piers and islands using size 2.5–3.5 Egi squid jigs.

#### 26. Snubnose Pompano (*Trachinotus blochii* | Bawal Emas)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 28°C – 31°C | **Depth:** 2m – 20m
- **Tactics:** Deep golden body on sandy flats and coral reefs. Target with live sea crabs or small jigs.
