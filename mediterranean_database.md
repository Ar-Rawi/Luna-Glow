## 1. Overview & Seasonal Filtering Engine

In the Mediterranean Sea, water temperatures fluctuate from **13°C in Winter** up to **27°C in Summer**, triggering massive seasonal migrations:
- **Winter (Dec–Feb):** Cold water species, deep reef jigs, cephalopods (squid/cuttlefish), and European Seabass.
- **Spring (Mar–May):** Inshore reef awakening, Dentex, Seabream, Cuttlefish, and Garfish.
- **Summer (Jun–Aug):** Surface thermocline formation, Bluefin Tuna, Barracuda, Pelagic trolling, Night Seabream.
- **Autumn (Sep–Nov):** **THE GOLDEN SEASON!** Surface baitfish frenzies, Greater Amberjack, Mahi-Mahi, False Albacore, Bluefish, and Bonito.

## 2. Exhaustive Species Catalog by Season & Habitat

### 🐟 Category A: Coastal Predators & Gamefish

#### 1. European Seabass (*Dicentrarchus labrax* | Spigola / Branzino / Loup de mer)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February (Autumn/Winter)
- **Ideal SST:** 14°C – 18°C | **Depth:** 1m – 20m
- **Tactics:** Target white-water surf zones and harbor mouths created by Mistral (NW) winds. Use 120mm minnow plugs, live prawns, or soft plastic paddle tails on slow retrieves.

#### 2. Gilthead Seabream (*Sparus aurata* | Orata / Dorade)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October (Late Spring to Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 18°C – 24°C | **Depth:** 2m – 25m
- **Tactics:** Target Posidonia seagrass margins and shellfish beds. Skittish bottom feeder with crushing jaws. Best fished on Full Moon nights with live shore crabs, bibi worms, or fresh mussels.

#### 3. Common Dentex (*Dentex dentex* | Dentice)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, September, October (Spring & Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 17°C – 21°C | **Depth:** 15m – 50m
- **Tactics:** Known as the "King of the Mediterranean Reef." Solitary apex hunter on steep rocky pinnacles. Slow-troll live squid or octopus near bottom structure.

#### 4. Bluefish (*Pomatomus saltatrix* | Serra / Anchoa)
- **Peak Months:** July, August, September, October, November (Summer to Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 20°C – 26°C | **Depth:** Surface – 15m
- **Tactics:** Razor-sharp teeth predator in coastal bays and river mouths. Use topwater needlefish plugs, poppers, or wire-leader live bait rigs.

#### 5. Leerfish / Garrick (*Lichia amia* | Leccia Amia)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October (Spring to Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 19°C – 25°C | **Depth:** 1m – 30m
- **Tactics:** Large coastal predator lurking in river estuaries and beach breakers. Fast topwater popping or live mullet trolling.

### 🐟 Category B: Pelagics & Deep Sea Gamefish

#### 6. Greater Amberjack (*Seriola dumerili* | Ricciola)
- **Peak Months:** September, October, November (Autumn Golden Season)
- **Ideal SST:** 19°C – 23°C | **Depth:** 20m – 100m
- **Tactics:** High-speed pack hunter on seamounts and offshore wrecks. Deep vertical metal jigging (150g–250g) or live squid trolling during Solunar Moon Overhead periods.

#### 7. Atlantic Bluefin Tuna (*Thunnus thynnus* | Tonno Rosso)
- **Peak Months:** June, July, August, September, October, November (Summer/Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 20°C – 26°C | **Depth:** Surface – 200m
- **Tactics:** Drift chumming (*Drifting*) with sardines or heavy surface popping into feeding frenzies (*Mangianze*).

#### 8. Mahi-Mahi / Dolphinfish (*Coryphaena hippurus* | Lampuga / Cannizzo)
- **Peak Months:** August, September, October, November (Late Summer & Autumn)
- **Ideal SST:** 22°C – 26°C | **Depth:** Surface – 30m
- **Tactics:** Targets floating FADs (*Cannizzi*) and debris. Light tackle trolling with feathers, small skirts, or live silversides.

#### 9. Little Tunny / False Albacore (*Euthynnus alletteratus* | Tonnetto Letterato)
- **Peak Months:** August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 20°C – 25°C | **Depth:** Surface – 50m
- **Tactics:** Fast surface shoals in autumn. Cast small 20g–40g silver metal casting jigs into boiling water.

#### 10. Atlantic Bonito (*Sarda sarda* | Palamita)
- **Peak Months:** August, September, October, November, December
- **Ideal SST:** 18°C – 24°C | **Depth:** 5m – 40m
- **Tactics:** Striped pelagic with sharp teeth. Trolling minnow plugs at 4–5 knots or light jigging around coastal headlands.

#### 11. Mediterranean Swordfish (*Xiphias gladius* | Pesce Spada)
- **Peak Months:** June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 21°C – 26°C | **Depth:** 100m – 600m
- **Tactics:** Deep drop night drifting with squid and cyalume glow sticks along continental shelf drop-offs.

### 🐟 Category C: Reef & Rock Bream (Sparidae Family)

#### 12. White Seabream (*Diplodus sargus* | Sarago Maggiore)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 17°C – 24°C | **Depth:** 1m – 30m
- **Tactics:** Heavy surf and rocky ledges. Light rock fishing (LRF) or bottom float fishing with sea worms and shrimp.

#### 13. Red Porgy / Pink Seabream (*Pagrus pagrus* | Pagro)
- **Peak Months:** April, May, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 16°C – 22°C | **Depth:** 20m – 80m
- **Tactics:** Deep rocky bottoms. Tai Kabura jigs, inchiku, or bottom ledgering with squid tentacles.

#### 14. Sharpsnout Seabream (*Diplodus puntazzo* | Sarago Pizzuto)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September
- **Ideal SST:** 18°C – 24°C | **Depth:** 2m – 20m
- **Tactics:** Algae and invertebrate feeder on cliff faces. Delicate float tackle with bread paste or shrimp.

#### 15. Striped Seabream (*Lithognathus mormyrus* | Mormora)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 19°C – 25°C | **Depth:** 2m – 15m
- **Tactics:** Sandy beaches at night. Surfcasting with Arenicola worms and light long leaders.

#### 16. Black Seabream (*Spondyliosoma cantharus* | Tanuta)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February, March
- **Ideal SST:** 14°C – 18°C | **Depth:** 15m – 60m
- **Tactics:** Winter reef swarms. Light boat bottom ledgering with squid strips and small hooks.

#### 17. Saddled Seabream (*Oblada melanura* | Occhiata)
- **Peak Months:** May, June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 18°C – 25°C | **Depth:** Surface – 15m
- **Tactics:** Surface schooling predator with black tail spot. Light topwater micro-poppers or bread float fishing.

---

### 🐟 Category D: Groupers & Deep Reef Predators

#### 18. Dusky Grouper (*Epinephelus marginatus* | Cernia Bruno)
- **Peak Months:** June, July, August, September, October
- **Ideal SST:** 20°C – 25°C | **Depth:** 10m – 60m
- **Tactics:** Large rocky caves and boulders. Heavy bottom baiting with live octopus or large cutlet. Lock drag to prevent burrowing.

#### 19. White Grouper (*Epinephelus aeneus* | Cernia Bianca)
- **Peak Months:** June, July, August, September, October, November
- **Ideal SST:** 19°C – 25°C | **Depth:** 20m – 80m
- **Tactics:** Sand/rock mixed bottoms. Slow pitch jigging or live bait bottom fishing.

#### 20. John Dory (*Zeus faber* | Pesce San Pietro)
- **Peak Months:** November, December, January, February, March, April
- **Ideal SST:** 13°C – 16°C | **Depth:** 30m – 150m
- **Tactics:** Cold water winter predator. Stalks live small fish. Target with live bogue/horse mackerel on long bottom leaders.

---

### 🦑 Category E: Cephalopods & Night Operations

#### 21. European Squid (*Loligo vulgaris* | Calamaro)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February, March
- **Ideal SST:** 13°C – 17°C | **Depth:** 5m – 50m
- **Tactics:** **Winter Night Peak!** Lighted harbor piers or slow boat drift using Egi squid jigs (size 2.5–3.5).

#### 22. Common Cuttlefish (*Sepia officinalis* | Seppia)
- **Peak Months:** February, March, April, May (Spring Spawn) & Oct–Dec
- **Ideal SST:** 14°C – 19°C | **Depth:** 2m – 25m
- **Tactics:** Sandy seagrass bottoms. Dragging weighted egi jigs slowly along sea floor.

#### 23. Common Octopus (*Octopus vulgaris* | Polpo)
- **Peak Months:** October, November, December, January, February, March, April
- **Ideal SST:** 14°C – 20°C | **Depth:** 1m – 30m
- **Tactics:** Rocky crevices and breakwaters. Octopus crabs jigs or white cloth handlines.
